# EcoStream Academic & Literature References

This project builds upon recent research in sustainable computing, carbon-aware workload orchestration, and edge AI adaptive inference. The following papers provide the theoretical foundation and empirical context for carbon-responsive scheduling and runtime model switching:

1. **Carbon-aware federated orchestration in edge-cloud systems: A multi-objective optimization framework with dynamic spatio-temporal workload management**
   - **Authors:** Ameer A. Al-Shammaa, Doaa H. Al-Hadrawi
   - **Venue:** *Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems*, Elsevier, Volume 51, Article 101398
   - **Year:** 2026
   - **DOI/URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.suscom.2026.101398](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.suscom.2026.101398)
   - **Relevance:** Formulates dynamic adaptive weighting responding to real-time grid carbon intensity and workload criticality across distributed nodes.

2. **CARA: Adaptive scheduling for carbon-efficient workload orchestration in distributed cloud systems**
   - **Authors:** Kai Wen Liow, Ruizhuo Wang, Runna Zheng, Hossain Akter, Bangning Huang, Hema Subramaniam
   - **Venue:** *Sustainable Computing: Informatics and Systems*, Elsevier, Volume 51, Article 101407
   - **Year:** 2026
   - **DOI/URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.suscom.2026.101407](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.suscom.2026.101407)
   - **Relevance:** Demonstrates carbon-aware adaptive scheduling architecture, reducing emissions during regional carbon peaks.

3. **Carbon-Aware Large Language Models for Edge Inference: Towards Sustainable and Adaptive AI**
   - **Authors:** Mary Valentina Janet A, Pavithra S, Monika S, Nishanthini T, Preethi Jenitha S, Kavya A
   - **Venue:** *2025 9th International Conference on Electronics, Communication and Aerospace Technology (ICECA)*, Coimbatore, India
   - **Publisher:** IEEE
   - **Year:** 2025 (Published in IEEE Xplore February 2026)
   - **DOI/URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1109/ICECA66444.2025.11382721](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICECA66444.2025.11382721)
   - **Relevance:** Adapts inference fidelity and model execution based on live grid carbon intensity signals at the edge.
